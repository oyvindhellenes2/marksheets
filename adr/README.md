# Architecture decision records

One record per decision that would be annoying to re-litigate, or costly to reverse. Not one per
change.

A record is **written once and then left alone**. If a decision is replaced, the new record says what
it supersedes and the old one is marked superseded rather than edited — the point of these files is
what was true and what was known at the time.

They divide with the other documents like this:

| | Holds | Tense |
|---|---|---|
| [`../SPEC.md`](../SPEC.md) | why the design **is** the way it is | present, edited freely |
| `adr/` | why **not** the alternatives, and what was true at the time | past, immutable once accepted |
| [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) | how to work here, and what not to break | imperative |

So SPEC states a rule and links here for the argument behind it. Statuses are **Proposed**,
**Accepted**, **Superseded** and **Rejected**; a rejection is worth recording whenever someone is
likely to propose the same thing again.
